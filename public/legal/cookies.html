Cookie policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. Version française

Cookies

A Studio sets no cookies, neither in the Android app nor on this site. No advertising, analytics or social media cookies.

Local storage

The app uses the device or browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) to save your projects when you ask, restore the open project, keep the samples, plugins and themes you install, and remember your preferences.

KeyContentPurpose
astudio:currentThe open projectRestore your work on reopening
astudio:projectsSaved projects, with name and dateList and reopen your projects
IndexedDB database "astudio"Samples you importUse them in your projects
astudio:themes, astudio:pluginsInstalled themes and pluginsUse them in the app
astudio:presetsPlugin presets you saveReload your sounds
astudio:ext:<plugin id>Data an extension keeps with the storage permission (100 KB max)Let the extension remember its settings; deleted with the extension
astudio:lang, astudio:theme, astudio:autoUpdateCheckLanguage, theme and update check choicesRespect your choices

This data is never transmitted. It is strictly necessary for the service you ask for. Under the French data protection authority (CNIL) guidelines, such storage does not require consent, which is why the app shows no cookie banner: there is nothing to accept or refuse.

Erasing this data

  • On Android: Settings, Apps, A Studio, Storage, Clear data.
  • In a browser: delete the site data in the browser privacy settings.
  • Item by item: delete buttons in the browser panel and in Settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. A Studio adds no third party scripts to these pages. As the host, GitHub may process technical connection data. See the privacy policy.
