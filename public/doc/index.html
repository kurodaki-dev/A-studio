A Studio documentation

A music production studio for Android, built on the FL Studio workflow: channel rack, piano roll, playlist, mixer and a browser for your samples.

Download the APK Try the web version

Install

  1. On your phone, download A-Studio.apk from the latest release.
  2. Open the file. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time.
  3. A Studio runs in landscape and full screen. Swipe from an edge to show the system bars.

Requirements: Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer. The app keeps itself up to date, see Updates.

Interface

The top bar holds the menu (three lines), the PAT and SONG modes, play, stop, record, metronome, tempo with a tap tempo button (tap it in rhythm, 2 taps or more), the position display (bar:step:tick), the current pattern and undo/redo. The rail on the left opens the browser and switches between the playlist, channel rack, piano roll and mixer.

Knobs and faders move with a vertical drag. A double tap resets them. The current value shows in the hint bar at the top right on wide screens.

Channel rack

  • Each row is a channel: enable light, pan, volume, mixer insert, name and 16 steps per bar.
  • Tap a step to turn it on; drag across steps to fill or clear several.
  • Step velocity: turn on the velocity button in the rack header, then drag up or down on a step to set its velocity. The orange bar shows it.
  • Channels can get a color and a group from the channel menu. When groups exist, a list in the rack header shows only the channels of one group.
  • The name button opens the channel menu: piano roll, settings, rename, clone, move, delete.
  • The same menu has Solo (mutes every other channel; choose it again to unmute them), Fill each 2, 4 or 8 steps, Random steps and Shift one step left or right (notes wrap around the pattern).
  • A channel holding piano roll notes shows a preview instead of steps. Tap it to open the piano roll.
  • The plus and minus buttons in the rack header add or remove bars from the pattern.
  • The Swing knob delays every odd 1/16 step of the pattern for a shuffled groove (up to half a step at 100%). It applies to playback, WAV and MIDI export.

Piano roll

The piano roll follows FL Studio. Notes are named like FL Studio: middle C is C5.

  • A long press on a note deletes it (the mouse right button does the same).
  • Scroll with the scrollbars at the bottom and right, with two fingers, or with the Pan view tool. Pinch to zoom: horizontally or vertically depending on how you hold your fingers.
  • Notes from the other channels show as ghost notes. The velocity lane edits note velocity.
  • Snap goes from 1/6 step to a full bar, or off.
  • Toolbar actions: copy, paste, duplicate, delete selection, transpose by a semitone or an octave, quantize, select all.
  • Scale: the scale button (note icon) highlights a scale. Pick a root note and one of 11 scales (major, minor, harmonic and melodic minor, modes, pentatonics, blues). Rows outside the scale are darker and the root rows are lighter. The scale is saved with the project.
  • Tools (wand icon) apply to the selected notes, or to every note of the channel when nothing is selected:
    • Chord: each note becomes the root of a chord (major, minor, diminished, augmented, sus2, sus4, 7th chords, minor 9, power chord).
    • Arpeggiate: the notes of each chord play one after the other at the snap length, up, down or up and down.
    • Strum: each note of a chord starts a little after the previous one.
    • Humanize: small random changes of velocity and timing.
    • Legato: each note lasts until the next one.
    • Reverse, Flip (mirror the pitches), Double length, Half length.
    • Snap notes to scale: moves out of scale notes to the nearest note of the scale.
  • Slide notes: Tools, Slide notes on or off. A slide note (triangle on its left) glides from the pitch of the previous note of the channel, over the Slide time set in the channel settings. Use it for 808 glides.
  • Live recording: turn on the Record button (red circle) in the top bar, start the pattern in PAT mode and play on the piano roll keyboard. Each note is written at the play position, snapped to the grid, with the length you held it.

Playlist

  • PAT plays the current pattern, SONG plays the playlist.
  • With the Draw tool, tap an empty spot to place the current pattern. Tap a clip to make its pattern current. Drag a clip to move it. Right click or use the Delete tool to delete clips.
  • A long press on a clip opens its menu: properties (audio clips), edit (automation clips), split here, duplicate, loop this clip, delete.
  • Select tool: tap clips or drag a box to select several, then drag one of them to move them all. The toolbar copies, pastes at the play position and deletes the selection (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+B to duplicate, Delete).
  • Slice tool: tap a clip to cut it in two at the snap position. The right part keeps playing from where it was in the pattern or sample.
  • Loop region: with the Select tool, drag on the ruler. The loop button turns it on or off; in SONG mode playback repeats the region.
  • Markers: the flag button adds a marker at the play position (or long press on the ruler). Tap a marker to jump to it, rename it, delete it or set a tempo change: from that marker the song plays at the new tempo, in playback and in exports.
  • Audio clips: Clip properties sets the gain, pitch (resampling, which also changes the speed), fade in, fade out and start offset.
  • Stretch a clip by dragging its right edge. A stretched pattern clip repeats its pattern; an audio clip is cut or extended with silence.
  • Tap a track name to mute the track.

Automation

An automation clip moves one parameter while the song plays: the volume, pan or filter of a channel, or the volume, pan, sends or EQ of a mixer insert.

  • In the playlist, tap the automation button (wave icon) and pick the parameter. A clip is placed on a free track over the length of the song.
  • In the editor, tap to add a point, drag a point to move it, long press or right click a point to delete it. Shapes give a ramp up or down, sine, saw or square curves in time with the tempo.
  • Tap the clip with the Draw tool to edit it again. Move, stretch, split, copy and delete it like any clip.
  • Automation plays in SONG mode and is rendered in WAV, MP3 and stem exports. When playback stops, the parameter goes back to its value in the mixer or channel settings.

Mixer

The master and 16 inserts each have a fader (0 dB at 80%), pan, reverb and delay sends, a three band equalizer (L, M, H: low shelf at 120 Hz, peak at 1 kHz, high shelf at 6 kHz, plus or minus 18 dB), mute, solo, a level meter and an FX button with 4 slots for effect plugins. Route a channel to an insert from the insert number in the channel rack or from the channel settings.

  • Routing: the arrow button under each insert sends it to the master or to a higher numbered insert, used as a bus (for example all drums into insert 10 with one compressor).
  • Built in effects for the FX slots: Compressor, Limiter, Filter (low pass, high pass, band pass, notch), Distortion (soft, hard, fold) and Sidechain. Each has presets.
  • Sidechain: put it on the insert to duck (a bass, for example) and choose the channel that triggers it (the kick by default). Every note of that channel lowers the insert by the Depth and brings it back over the Release time.

Browser and samples

  • Open or close the browser with the top button of the left rail (F8 on a keyboard). It has a search field and folders for projects, your samples, plugins and built in sounds.
  • The import button accepts audio files (WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC, M4A, AIFF) and ZIP sample packs. Folders inside a pack are kept. Samples are stored on the device only.
  • Tap an item to preview it. To drop it somewhere, drag it to the right or long press it, then move it:
    • onto a playlist track: it becomes an audio clip with its waveform;
    • into the piano roll: a Sampler channel is created and a note is placed where you let go;
    • into the channel rack: a Sampler channel is created.
  • The Sampler plays the sample at its own pitch on C5 and transposes it on other notes. Its channel settings have Play reversed, Start and End points, and Loop while the note is held (otherwise the sample plays to its end).
  • Chop (slicer): cut the sample into 4, 8 or 16 equal slices, or Auto (cuts on the hits). C5 plays the first slice, C#5 the second and so on, and a pattern that replays the loop is written, ready to rearrange in the piano roll.
  • Multi sample instrument: on a folder of the browser, Make an instrument from this folder. Name the samples after their note (Piano C4.wav, Piano F#4.wav, or the MIDI number Piano 60.wav; packs name middle C "C4"). Each key plays the nearest sample, transposed.
  • Every channel has a full envelope: attack, decay, sustain, release.

Recording and MIDI

  • The Record button (red circle) opens a menu: Notes arms note recording (piano roll keyboard and MIDI keyboard), Audio from the microphone records into the playlist, and Count in one bar plays one bar of metronome before recording starts.
  • Microphone: playback starts in SONG mode from the play position (after the count in) and Stop places the take as an audio clip on a free track. Takes are kept in the browser under Recordings. Android asks for the microphone permission the first time. Use headphones so the song is not recorded with your voice.
  • MIDI keyboard: connect a USB MIDI keyboard. It plays the channel open in the piano roll, with velocity, and records notes while recording is armed. Turn it on or off in Settings. Web MIDI depends on the device: Settings shows when it is not supported.
  • Import MIDI file (menu): a .mid file becomes a new pattern with one channel per track and the tempo of the file. Drums on MIDI channel 10 are split into the built in drum sounds.

Projects and export

  • New project offers templates: empty, hip hop, trap (with an 808 glide) and house.
  • Time signature (Project info): 2/4 to 7/4. Bars, the grid and the metronome follow it.
  • The top bar shows the voices playing and the load of the sequencer (CPU).
  • The open project is saved automatically. Use Save or Save as to keep named projects on the device.
  • Export project file writes a .astudio file you can share or back up. It does not contain imported samples: import the pack again on another device.
  • Export renders the pattern (PAT mode) or the song (SONG mode), 44.1 kHz stereo, as WAV 16 bit, WAV 24 bit, MP3 192 or 320 kbps, with optional normalization to -0.3 dB. MP3 encoding uses LAME (lamejs, LGPL), loaded as a separate file only when you export an MP3.
  • Export stems (ZIP) renders one WAV file per channel that has notes, plus one for the audio clips in SONG mode, all with the same length so they line up in another DAW. Muted channels are skipped.
  • Project info (in the menu) holds the project name, an author and free notes (lyrics, ideas), and counts channels, patterns, notes and clips.
  • Export as MIDI writes a standard .mid file of the pattern or the song, one track per channel, with the tempo. Drums use MIDI channel 10 and General MIDI drum notes; other channels get a close General MIDI program. Open it in FL Studio or any other DAW.

Settings and language

Menu, Settings. The interface is in English by default and also available in French. Settings also hold the theme, plugins, the update check, the documentation link and the bug report button.

Updates

Every change published on the main branch builds a signed APK and a new release (v1.0.<number>). At startup the app checks the latest release and offers it. Tap Download: the app downloads the APK, then Android shows its installer and asks you to confirm. Android requires that confirmation and the first time also asks you to allow A Studio to install apps. When the install finishes, tap Open to restart A Studio on the new version. Projects are kept.

The startup check can be turned off in Settings. Menu, Check for updates, checks by hand.

Plugins

A plugin is a JavaScript file. There are three kinds:

  • Instruments generate sound for notes, sample by sample: oscillators, filters, envelopes, physical models, anything you can compute. They appear in the browser under Plugins and work like any instrument: channel rack, piano roll, playlist, WAV export.
  • Effects (@type effect) process the sound of a mixer insert: distortion, delay, filters, compressors. Each insert has 4 effect slots behind its FX button.
  • Extensions (@type extension) change the interface: buttons in the top bar, menu items, and whole panels with your own HTML, CSS and JavaScript, opened from the left rail. They can drive playback and read or write the project. See Extensions.

Install a plugin from Menu, Plugins and themes: Install a plugin picks a file on the phone, Install from GitHub takes a link (see Install from GitHub), or write one directly on the phone with Write a plugin: the editor has an instrument template and an effect template, checks the header as you go, installs the plugin and plays a test chord, and exports the file to share it. Installed plugins can be edited again with the pencil button.

Where the code runs

Plugin code runs only inside an AudioWorklet, the audio thread of the app. That scope has no access to the interface, to storage (projects, samples, settings), to the network or to files: a plugin receives notes and parameter values and returns samples. The host also protects the app:

  • an exception in a voice removes that voice and shows the error once, the app keeps running;
  • output is limited to ±4 and NaN values become silence;
  • 32 voices at most per channel, and a voice still playing 10 seconds after its note off is cut.

A plugin can still use a lot of CPU and make the sound stutter. Install plugins from people you trust.

A minimal plugin

/**
 * @astudio-plugin
 * @id simple-sine
 * @name Simple Sine
 * @version 1.0.0
 * @author Your name
 * @category synth
 * @param release "Release" 0.01 2 0.3
 */
class SineVoice {
  // Called when a note starts.
  constructor({ key, frequency, velocity, sampleRate, params }) {
    this.step = frequency / sampleRate;
    this.phase = 0;
    this.gain = 0.2 * velocity;
    this.sr = sampleRate;
    this.releasing = false;
  }

  // Called when the note ends (optional).
  noteOff() {
    this.releasing = true;
  }

  // Fill left and right (Float32Array, up to 128 samples, already zeroed).
  // Return false when the voice is finished.
  process(left, right, params) {
    for (let i = 0; i < left.length; i++) {
      if (this.releasing) this.gain *= Math.exp(-1 / (params.release * this.sr));
      const s = Math.sin(2 * Math.PI * this.phase) * this.gain;
      this.phase = (this.phase + this.step) % 1;
      left[i] = s;
      right[i] = s;
    }
    return !(this.releasing && this.gain < 0.0001);
  }
}

registerVoice(SineVoice);

Presets

Declare factory presets in the header. Each one sets some controls; the others keep their value.

 * @preset "Warm pad" wave=0 cutoff=1200 reso=0.2 attack=0.6
 * @preset "Acid" wave=0 cutoff=700 reso=0.9

A preset list appears above the controls, in the channel settings and in the FX window. Save as preset keeps your own settings on the device, per plugin; they can be deleted again.

A file is limited to 256 KB. Use plain JavaScript in one file; import of other files is not available.

Effects

/**
 * @astudio-plugin
 * @id soft-clip
 * @name Soft Clip
 * @type effect
 * @param drive "Drive" 1 20 4
 */
class SoftClip {
  constructor({ sampleRate, params }) {}

  // inL, inR: input samples. Write the result in outL, outR.
  process(inL, inR, outL, outR, params) {
    for (let i = 0; i < inL.length; i++) {
      outL[i] = Math.tanh(inL[i] * params.drive);
      outR[i] = Math.tanh(inR[i] * params.drive);
    }
  }
}

registerEffect(SoftClip);
ItemDescription
registerEffect(EffectClass)Required for effects. One instance per slot.
constructor({ sampleRate, params })Called when the effect is placed in a slot.
process(inL, inR, outL, outR, params, transport)Called for every block of 128 samples. Read the input, write the output. transport gives { bpm, playing, beat } for tempo synced effects.
button(id, params)Optional. Called when a @button is pressed.

If an effect throws an error, its slot lets the sound through unchanged and the error is shown once.

Plugin catalog

Menu, Plugins and themes, Browse the catalog lists plugins and themes shared by the community, with a search field and filters by kind. One tap installs or reinstalls an item. The list is the file public/plugins/catalog.json of the repository: to add yours, open a pull request that adds an entry (see the plugins folder).

Install from GitHub

Paste the link of a public repository, folder or file in Menu, Plugins and themes, Install from GitHub. The app lists the plugins and themes it finds and installs the ones you choose. Accepted links:

https://github.com/user/repo/tree/main/some/folderThat folder
https://github.com/user/repo/blob/main/my-synth.astudio-plugin.jsThat file
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/user/repo/main/my-synth.astudio-plugin.jsThat file

Plugins installed this way remember where they come from: the Update from GitHub button next to them downloads the latest version. Try it with the examples of this documentation: https://github.com/kurodaki-dev/A-studio/tree/main/public/doc/examples.

Publish your plugin

  1. Create a public GitHub repository.
  2. Name your files something.astudio-plugin.js (plugins) or something.astudio-theme.json (themes) and put them at the root or in a plugins folder.
  3. Share the repository link. To ship an update, push the new file and raise @version.

Extensions

An extension adds interface to the app. Its code runs in an isolated frame (a sandboxed iframe with no origin): it cannot read the app page, its storage, your projects on disk, your samples or your files. It talks to the app only through the astudio API, and the app refuses any call that needs a permission the header does not declare.

/**
 * @astudio-plugin
 * @id play-button
 * @name Play Button
 * @type extension
 * @permissions ui transport
 */

// A button in the top bar. The second argument runs on every tap.
astudio.ui.addButton({ id: 'play', label: 'Play', icon: 'Play' }, () => astudio.transport.toggle());

// Keep the label in sync with playback.
astudio.on('state', (s) => {
  astudio.ui.addButton({ id: 'play', label: s.playing ? 'Stop' : 'Play', icon: s.playing ? 'Square' : 'Play' }, () => astudio.transport.toggle());
});

Examples

  • ai-connect.astudio-plugin.js: connects A Studio to an AI through the astudio-mcp bridge. Adds the AI connection section in Settings and an AI indicator in the top bar. Uses settings, network, notes, channels and transport.
  • chord-helper.astudio-plugin.js: extension with a Chords panel: pick a key, tap chords to hear them and build a progression, write it as block chords or arpeggios, save progressions. Uses notes, audio, storage, project events and an Alt+C shortcut.
  • beat-tools.astudio-plugin.js: extension with a Play/Stop button in the top bar, a menu item and a Beat generator panel (styles, density, tap tempo) that writes a beat into the pattern.
  • poly-synth.astudio-plugin.js: resonant filter synth that uses every control type: choice, knobs, switch and button.
  • stereo-delay.astudio-plugin.js: effect, delay synced to the tempo (or free), feedback, tone, ping pong, Clear button and presets.
  • bitcrusher.astudio-plugin.js: effect, bit depth and sample rate reduction.
  • supersaw.astudio-plugin.js: seven detuned saws, filter and envelope, three knobs.
  • fm-bell.astudio-plugin.js: two operator FM bell.
  • noise-snare.astudio-plugin.js: a one shot drum made of a tone and filtered noise.

Projects store plugin channels as plugin:<id> with their knob values. If the plugin is missing on a device, the channel stays silent until you install it.

Connect an AI (MCP)

An AI assistant such as Claude can work in A Studio: read the project, write notes and chords, add channels, set the tempo, play. It goes through the Model Context Protocol (MCP):

AI app (Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor...)  -- MCP -->  astudio-mcp on your computer  -- local network -->  A Studio
  1. On your computer, install the bridge from the mcp-server folder (Node.js 20+), then add it to your AI app with a pairing code. The bridge README has the exact configuration for Claude Desktop, Claude Code and others.
  2. In A Studio, install the AI Connect (MCP) plugin from the catalog. A new AI connection (MCP) section appears in Settings, and an AI indicator in the top bar.
  3. In that section, enter the bridge address (ws:// + the computer's local IP + :8765, printed by the bridge) and the pairing code, then turn on Connect.
  4. Ask your AI, for example: "Add a bass channel in A Studio and write a four bar line in A minor, then play it."

The phone and the computer must be on the same network. What the AI reads from your project goes to the AI service you use. Every change it makes can be undone with Undo.

Privacy

A Studio collects no personal data. Projects, samples, plugins and themes stay on the device; plugin code runs isolated in the audio thread. The only connection checks GitHub for a new version and downloads it when you accept. Details: privacy policy, terms of use, cookie policy.

Building from source

Requirements: Node.js 22, JDK 21 and the Android SDK (platform 36).

git clone https://github.com/kurodaki-dev/A-studio.git
cd A-studio
npm install
npm run dev            # web version on http://localhost:5173
npm test               # unit tests
npm run build
npx cap sync android
cd android && ./gradlew assembleDebug

Found a problem? Open a bug report. An idea? Request a feature. Both are also in the app menu.
